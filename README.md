## What is AI Legal Assistant?

AI Legal Assistant is an AI-powered application designed to make legal information easier to access and understand. It uses **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)** combined with **Large Language Model (LLM) APIs** to answer legal queries based on relevant legal documents and contextual information.

Users can ask questions in natural language, while the system retrieves relevant content from its document knowledge base and provides it to the language model as context. The LLM then generates clear, relevant, and context-aware responses grounded in the retrieved information.

By combining document retrieval with generative AI, the application aims to reduce the effort required to search through lengthy legal documents and make legal information more accessible.

### Key Capabilities
- **Natural Language Queries:** Ask legal questions in everyday language.
- **Document-Based Retrieval:** Retrieve relevant information from the legal document knowledge base.
- **AI-Generated Responses:** Generate contextual answers using LLM APIs and retrieved content.
- **RAG Architecture:** Connect document retrieval with language model generation to provide more relevant responses.
- **Simplified Legal Information:** Help users understand complex legal content through conversational interaction.
